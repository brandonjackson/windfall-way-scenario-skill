## Key Parameters

### Horizon

<!-- Which planning horizon does this scenario inhabit?

- **Horizon 1**: Extension of the present. Familiar institutions, incremental
  change. Useful for near-term policy stress-testing.
- **Horizon 2**: Emerging change. Current institutions are strained or adapting.
  New dynamics are visible but not yet dominant. Typically 3–7 years out.
- **Horizon 3**: Transformative change. Fundamental shifts in organising
  principles — new kinds of economies, institutions, or social contracts.
  The world works differently. Can be any timeframe.

The horizon shapes the register of the scenario, not just the date it's
set in. A scenario set in 2030 could be H2 (strained institutions) or
H3 (transformed ones). -->

### AI Capability Assumptions

<!-- What can AI do in this world? Free text, but consider these reference
levels as scaffolding:

- **Augmentation**: AI assists human workers but can't perform multi-step
  tasks end-to-end without supervision. Powerful but tool-like. Entry-level
  roles are displaced; strategic and creative roles become more valuable.
- **Substitution**: AI can perform multi-hour cognitive tasks autonomously
  — writing briefs, building models, implementing features. Firms can
  reorganise work around AI. The threshold for delegation has shifted.
- **Full automation**: AI can perform nearly all cognitive and creative
  labour at or above human level. The economic rationale for employing
  humans in most traditional roles disappears.

These are not sharp categories. Describe the specific capabilities that
matter for this scenario's audience and setting. -->
