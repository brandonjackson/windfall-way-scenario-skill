## Policy Scenario Workshop

Senior policymakers (e.g. Whitehall civil servants, GO-Science) inhabit a
scenario and explore policy responses. Goal is to stress-test current
frameworks and surface blind spots. Typically half-day or full-day.

**Example:** DSIT workshop (March 2026) with "Transformation Economy" and
"Open Frontier" scenarios.

## Stakeholder Convening

Mixed group — academics, civil society, think tanks, possibly private sector —
brought together around a shared concern. More exploratory than a pure policy
workshop. Goal is to build shared language and identify intervention points.

**Example:** London workshop (February 2026) with Daniel Susskind, featuring
"White Collar Wipeout / Crossing the Threshold."

## Foresight Exercise

Shorter, more generative format. Participants explore multiple scenarios
quickly to map the possibility space rather than deep-dive into one. Often
used early in a research programme to set direction.

## Executive Briefing

Condensed format for time-poor senior leaders. One scenario, tightly
facilitated, focused on "what would you do?" rather than open exploration.
